Falling Pickaxe — iOS browser version
=====================================

How to play on iPhone / iPad:
1. Upload the falling-pickaxe-web folder to any HTTPS host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, etc.)
2. Open the site in Safari
3. Hold the phone vertically (portrait)
4. Optional: Share → Add to Home Screen for fullscreen app mode

Notes:
- Portrait orientation (vertical phone layout)
- First tap unlocks sound (Safari policy)
- Works offline after first load (service worker)
